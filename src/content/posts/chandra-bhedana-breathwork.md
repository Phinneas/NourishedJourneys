<p>Chandra Bhedana breathing is among the many types of yogic breathing that is also called pranayama breathing. Chandra Bhedana is the left nostril breathing technique. For a complete overview of all pranayama methods, see our <a href="/pranayama-breathing-techniques/">pranayama breathing techniques</a> guide.<a> Surya Bhedana</a> is the right nostril breathing which I wrote about earlier. Chandra also means moon</p><p>What you are doing is simply blocking the right nostril so you breathe only through your left nostril. You can sit comfortably or with your legs crossed on a pillow on the floor. However, you feel most comfortable is the key.</p><p>So you’re sitting on the couch a recliner or a pillow on the floor. I like to take the first finger of my left hand and reach over to my right nostril. The other way of using my thumb I find I slightly block my breathing through my left nostril. It is a little uncomfortable I think.</p><p>The benefits of left nostril breathing benefits have been shown scientifically. In<a> this</a> article, the researchers noted that left-nostril breathing had a reduction in sympathetic nervous system activity and an increase in the parasympathetic nervous system. By reducing the sympathetic nervous system we relax. This meditation-breathing technique will help you relax and turn off the fight-or-flight mode which is the sympathetic nervous system's job.</p><p>I did write earlier about alternate nostril breathing before<a> here</a>. It showed that alternate nostril breathing allowed a group of basketball players to increase their shooting accuracy. Interesting huh?</p><p>Thank you</p>
