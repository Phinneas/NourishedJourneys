<h2 id="understanding-divided-attention">Understanding Divided Attention</h2><p>Join us as we dive into the world of&nbsp;juggling divided attention&nbsp;and discover how we manage to do multiple things at once without losing our train of thought. It's basically our brain’s ability to handle a couple of different things at once. Think about chatting on the phone while whipping up dinner or cruising down the highway (<a>Fiveable</a>).</p><p>Divided attention is a big player in our lives, helping us juggle tasks when we need to.
